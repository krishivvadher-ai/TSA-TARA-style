Checking a paper

  1. Open the year, then the set's Answers.
  2. Mark your sheet against the answers. One mark each, no penalty for wrong answers.
  3. Tap any question you got wrong (or guessed) to jump straight to its worked solution.

Each set: 25 questions · 25 marks · 45 minutes. One mark per question and no penalty for a wrong answer. The time is scaled from TARA (22 questions in 40 minutes).
